HYTZ WEBSITE - WHAT WE FOUND AND WHAT WE CHANGED
================================================


PROBLEMS ON THE CURRENT WEBSITE (hytz.co.in)
--------------------------------------------
- "Let's talk", "More about us" and "Contact Now" buttons do nothing when clicked.
- Clicking anywhere on the big black opening screen opens a broken page.
- The "+" menu button at the top does nothing.
- The menu links at the bottom (Home, About, Work...) look clickable but aren't.
- The "Work" and "Insights" pages don't exist (page not found).
- The email shown is info@hytz.co.in, but clicking it emails "dummy@mail.com".
  Every enquiry sent that way is lost.
- The About and Contact pages are still the unedited website template:
  they talk about someone called "Clive", show prices in US dollars,
  claim awards Hytz never won, and give a Los Angeles address.
- "Use for Free" buttons link to the template seller's page.
- A large empty orange box sits near the bottom of the page.
- The impact numbers briefly show "0X" and "1M+" before loading properly.
- Images have no descriptions, which hurts Google ranking and accessibility.


WHAT THE NEW WEBSITE DOES BETTER
--------------------------------
- Same look: same black, off-white and orange colors, same logo, same fonts.
- Every button now works and goes somewhere useful.
- Working menu that opens full-screen and closes with the Esc key.
- Opening screen: same big "hytz." logo, plus clear "Start a project"
  and "See our work" buttons.
- Smooth "staircase" effect when scrolling from the opening screen,
  copied from the current site.
- Greeting in Malayalam (namaskaram) in grey with an orange "!".
- "Namaskaram" section uses the same layout and fonts as the current site.
- Client logos are bigger and scroll in an endless loop.
- New "Now Showing" work section: posters slide past like a film reel as you
  scroll, with the project name, details and results next to each one.
  On phones you swipe through them instead.
- The work headline has a playful "paused video" effect on the word "stopped".
- Click any poster to open a full case study, then go to the next one.
- Work can be filtered by Film, Brand or Events.
- Services open up to show what's included, with a "Discuss" button that
  ticks that service in the contact form for you.
- Impact numbers count up smoothly and never show "0".
- The empty orange box is now a big "Let's break the internet" call to action.
- Contact form checks for mistakes (like a wrong email) before sending.
- "Send enquiry" now emails info@hytz.co.in directly.
- A copy button next to the email address.
- WhatsApp, Call, Instagram, LinkedIn and Facebook buttons, plus a line to reach
  Antony Roy (CEO & Founder) directly on +91 97463 21230.
- New "Insights" section and its first article: the story of the Job Kurian
  website (turned from Antony's LinkedIn post into a proper page).
  This replaces the "Insights" link that was broken on the old site.
- Better Google results for the article: its own title, description,
  author, date and preview image, plus a sitemap so Google finds every page.
- An orange circle follows the mouse a little behind it. It turns dark on
  orange areas so it never disappears, and grows slightly over buttons.
- Buttons fill with color when clicked, then take you to the page.
- Works well on phones, and respects people who turn off animations.
- Better Google listing: proper page title, description and sharing preview.
