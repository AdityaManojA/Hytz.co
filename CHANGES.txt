IMPROVEMENTS

SEO: WHY IT MATTERS FOR SALES
What was added:
- Every page has a clear title and description written for Google.
- Rich previews when links are shared on WhatsApp, LinkedIn and X.
- Hidden "business card" data telling Google who Hytz is, where it is,
  the founder, phone number and social pages.
- The article is marked as a news-style story (author, date, image).
- A sitemap so Google finds and lists every page quickly.

How it brings in business:
- Found by the right people: when a brand or film producer searches
  "marketing agency Kerala" or "film promotion agency", a well-set-up
  site can appear in results instead of being invisible.
- Every article is a new door in: the Job Kurian story can show up for
  people searching for Job Kurian, artist websites or web design in
  Kerala, which brings visitors who never searched for "Hytz".
- Name searches look professional: searching "Hytz" can show the logo,
  contact details and social links together, which builds trust.
- Shares look polished: links sent on WhatsApp or LinkedIn show a proper
  image and headline, so more people click.
- Visitors become leads: once they arrive, every button now works and
  the form actually reaches the inbox, so the extra traffic turns into
  real enquiries instead of being lost.
- It keeps building: each new case study or article adds more ways to
  be found, at no extra advertising cost.

- "Now Showing" film-reel showcase of the work, with case studies.
- Staircase scroll effect, orange mouse circle, fill-on-click buttons.
- Client logos much bigger in an endless strip; hold the mouse on one
  for a second and it pops out larger, in full color, with the name.
- Built for phones: the work reel plays as you scroll (or swipe), swipe
  through case studies and down to close, drag the logo strip,
  press-and-hold a logo to zoom, and a floating WhatsApp /
  "Start a project" bar while browsing.
- Also works with a keyboard.
