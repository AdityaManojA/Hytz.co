    Tracking plan

    Implemented on the site
    EventTriggerPurpose
    generate_leadEnquiry form submittedPrimary conversion. Used by Meta and Google to optimise bidding.
    whatsapp_clickWhatsApp button tappedRecords WhatsApp enquiries, which would otherwise go unattributed.
    call_clickPhone number tappedAttributes phone enquiries to the source campaign.
    case_study_openProject openedShows which projects attract interest.
    service_interestService enquiry button tappedMeasures demand by service.
    form_startFirst input in the formCompared with submissions, shows form drop-off.
    work_reel_completeLast project in the work section reachedBuilds an engaged-visitor audience for retargeting.
    scroll_depth25, 50, 75 and 100% of the page scrolledMeasures how far visitors read.
    work_filter, cta_click, social_clickFilter, button or social link usedShows navigation patterns and exit points.

    Steps to go live

    • Install a Google Tag Manager container on the site.
    • Connect GA4, the Meta Pixel and the Meta Conversions API to the events above.
    • Set generate_lead, whatsapp_click and call_click as conversions in Meta and Google Ads.
    • Add a cookie consent banner, as required under India's DPDP Act.
    • Build the monthly report in Looker Studio for sharing with clients.

    Benefits for Hytz

    • Measured evidence for the 70% ROI claim.
    • Lower cost per lead, as ad platforms optimise for enquiries rather than clicks.
    • A packaged client service: tracking, landing pages and reporting.
    • Full measurement of film campaigns, from trailer views to ticket bookings.
