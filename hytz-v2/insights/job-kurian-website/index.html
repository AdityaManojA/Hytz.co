Website launch

Job Kurian's Official Website, Designed & Developed by Hytz.

A milestone project for our team: the digital home for one of the icons of Kerala's independent music scene.

By Antony Roy, Founder & CEO · 8 December 2025 · 2 min read

We'd been quietly building something special. On 7 December 2025, at 4pm, we hit Launch.

Job Kurian's official website, designed and developed by Hytz, is now live at jobkurian.com.

Why this one mattered

We're incredibly grateful for the opportunity to craft the digital space for one of the icons of Kerala's independent music scene. And, more personally, for someone whose music finds a place in every one of our playlists.

What's on the site

The site brings Job Kurian's music and story together in one place:

  • Original compositions: the songs that carry his own story.
  • Screen melodies: his playback work for Indian cinema.
  • Live music: his on-stage performances, with a dedicated JK Live page.
  • Job Kurian's 10: his dream project of ten songs and ten chapters of his life.
  • Latest releases and accolades, plus his journey and a way to connect.

The people behind it

A heartfelt thank you to the team behind this project, Awin Anto, Sagar Sreekumar Pillai and Ali S, and to Athira Job for guiding us through it.

And to the powerhouse team at Hytz: Arathy Varier, Varun Sankar A, Anand K, Mohammed Rashid, Muhammed Yaseen PM and Aldrin. Thank you!
