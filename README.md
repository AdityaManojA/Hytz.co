# Hytz website: rebuilt

A rebuild of [hytz.co.in](https://www.hytz.co.in/) that keeps the brand's look (black, off-white, Hytz orange, the "hytz." wordmark, the Urbanist and Geist fonts) and fixes how the site works.

**Live preview:** https://hytz-co.web.app

---

## Issues on the current site

**Buttons and links**
- "Let's talk", "More about us" and "Contact Now" do nothing when clicked.
- The whole opening screen is a link to a broken address, so clicking anywhere on it opens a dead tab.
- The "+" menu button does nothing.
- The footer menu (Home, About, Work…) looks clickable but isn't.
- The "Work" and "Insights" pages don't exist (page not found).
- The email shows **info@hytz.co.in** but actually opens **dummy@mail.com**, so every email enquiry is lost.

**Leftover template content**
- The About page talks about "Clive", lists prices in US dollars and claims awards Hytz never won.
- The Contact page says "Contact Me", gives a **Los Angeles address** and has the template's own FAQ and social links.
- "Use for Free" buttons link to the template seller.

**Everything else**
- A large empty orange box above the footer.
- Impact numbers briefly show "0X" and "1M+" before loading.
- No main heading, and almost no images have descriptions, which hurts Google ranking and accessibility.
- No case studies, even though the film work is the strongest proof Hytz has.

---

## What the new site does better

**Everything works**
- Every button goes somewhere real: no dead clicks anywhere, and "Back to top" really goes to the top.
- A full-screen menu that opens and closes (Esc key, or a swipe on phones).
- The email link goes to info@hytz.co.in, with a one-click "copy email" button.
- "Send enquiry" emails info@hytz.co.in directly, checks for mistakes first and confirms when sent.
- WhatsApp, Call, Instagram, LinkedIn and Facebook buttons, plus a direct line to Antony Roy, CEO & Founder.

**Same look, more life**
- The same big "hytz." opening screen, now with clear "Start a project" and "See our work" buttons.
- The "staircase" scroll effect from the current site, recreated.
- The greeting in Malayalam (നമസ്കാരം!) and the same "Namaskaram" layout and fonts as the current site.
- Much bigger client logos in an endless strip; hold on a logo for a second and it pops out larger, in full color, with the client's name.
- An orange circle that follows the mouse, changes color so it never disappears, and shows "View" over the posters.
- Buttons fill with color when clicked, then take you to the page.
- An orange square browser-tab icon.

**Work and stories**
- A cinematic "Now Showing" reel: posters slide past like film as you scroll, with project details beside each one.
- Each poster opens a case study; work can be filtered by Film, Brand or Events.
- Services open up to show what's included, and "Discuss" pre-selects that service in the contact form.
- The empty orange box is now a bold "Let's break the internet" call to action.
- A new **Insights** section, starting with the story of the Job Kurian website, which shows Hytz builds websites as well as campaigns.

**Built for phones**
- The work reel pins and plays as you scroll, just like on desktop; swiping left/right also moves it.
- Swipe case studies left/right, and down to close.
- Drag or flick the logo strip; press and hold a logo to zoom in.
- A floating WhatsApp / "Start a project" bar while browsing, which steps aside near the contact form.
- Fits around the notch and home bar on modern phones, with no stuck hover effects after a tap.

**Found on Google, works for everyone**
- Proper page titles, descriptions and share previews for WhatsApp, LinkedIn and X.
- Business and article details marked up for Google, plus a sitemap.
- Supports keyboard use, and calms the animations for people who turn motion off.
